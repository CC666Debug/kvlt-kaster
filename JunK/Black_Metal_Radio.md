## The subgenres

| Style | What it sounds like | Key bands |
|---|---|---|
| **First Wave** | Early 80s proto-black: thrashy, evil, lo-fi | Venom, Bathory, Hellhammer, Celtic Frost, Mercyful Fate |
| **Second Wave / Norwegian** | Tremolo riffs, blast beats, shrieks, cold raw production (early 90s) | Mayhem, Darkthrone, Burzum, Emperor, Immortal, Gorgoroth |
| **Raw Black Metal** | Deliberately harsh, trebly, lo-fi recordings | Ildjarn, Paysage d'Hiver, Darkthrone (Transilvanian Hunger) |
| **Atmospheric Black Metal** | Long, hypnotic songs, nature themes, keyboards and ambience | Wolves in the Throne Room, Summoning, Agalloch, Darkspace |
| **Symphonic Black Metal** | Orchestras and keyboards over black metal | Emperor, Dimmu Borgir, Cradle of Filth, Limbonic Art |
| **Melodic Black Metal** | Catchier, melodic riffing | Dissection, Naglfar, Sacramentum, Watain |
| **Depressive / Suicidal (DSBM)** | Slow, despairing, howled vocals | Xasthur, Shining, Silencer, Lifelover, Nocturnal Depression |
| **Pagan / Viking / Folk Black** | Heathen themes, folk instruments, epic choirs | Enslaved, Moonsorrow, Windir, Primordial, Drudkh |
| **Orthodox / Religious Black** | Theological, occult, liturgical and dissonant | Deathspell Omega, Watain, Ofermod, Funeral Mist |
| **War Metal / Bestial** | Chaotic, blasting black-death noise | Blasphemy, Revenge, Archgoat, Beherit |
| **Black Thrash / Black Speed** | Black metal with thrash and punk energy | Aura Noir, Nifelheim, Deströyer 666, Midnight |
| **Blackened Death** | Death metal heaviness with black metal atmosphere | Behemoth, Belphegor, Dissection, Necrophobic |
| **Post-Black / Blackgaze** | Black metal mixed with shoegaze and post-rock | Alcest, Deafheaven, Harakiri for the Sky, Lantlôs |
| **Cascadian Black Metal** | US Pacific Northwest: forests, ecology, post-rock | Wolves in the Throne Room, Fauna, Skagos |
| **Avant-garde / Experimental** | Strange structures and unusual instruments | Ved Buens Ende, Arcturus, Solefald, Oranssi Pazuzu, Blut Aus Nord |
| **Blackened Doom / Funeral** | Crushingly slow black-tinged doom | Barathrum, Bethlehem, Evoken, Skepticism |
| **Dungeon Synth (sister genre)** | Medieval-fantasy keyboard ambience, born from black metal intros | Mortiis, Burzum (prison albums), Old Tower, Fief |
