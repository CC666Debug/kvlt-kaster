# 🐐 Kvlt Kaster

A free phone remote for **black metal** internet radio: pure black metal, extreme metal, pagan and Viking, blackened doom and avant-garde. Listen on your phone, or cast to Google Nest speakers.

**▶ Open the app: https://cc666debug.github.io/kvlt-kaster/**

Sister app to [🎃 Halloween Caster](https://cc666debug.github.io/halloween-caster/), [🌆 Synth Caster](https://cc666debug.github.io/synth-caster/) and [🌙 Pagan Caster](https://cc666debug.github.io/pagan-caster/).

## What it does

- **170 stations**, every one tested: Svartkrog, True Black Metal Radio, Ritus, Satanic Radio, Death.FM, The Pit (NTS), Radio Caprice's black metal channels and more, plus a 🤘 Metal Mix of general metal stations.
- **Moon phase and the next unholy date**: Friday the 13th, Walpurgisnacht, 6/6 and Samhain.
- **Song names** on stations marked ♪, on screen and on your lock screen.
- **Search, genre filters, sorting, shuffle and favorites.**
- **Saved songs.** Tap 🤍 while a song plays to keep it, then find it later on YouTube, YouTube Music or Spotify. Back it up to a folder once and every save or removal is copied there by itself, in a list file of this device's own (phone, tablet or PC), so devices sharing a synced folder never overwrite each other.
- **Share the app** with the button at the bottom.
- **Cast to speakers** with the cast button (Chrome on Android or a computer).
- **Opens instantly, even offline**, once added to your home screen.

No accounts. Favorites, saved songs and settings stay on your device. A cookie-free counter (GoatCounter) counts visits to the page; nothing personal is collected.

Streams belong to their stations. Hail the underground.
